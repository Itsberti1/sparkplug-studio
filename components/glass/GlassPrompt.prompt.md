Liquid-glass voice question bubble with a waveform button — float over photography only.
```jsx
<GlassPrompt text="Hey Williams, who is the current backup driver?" side="left" tilt={16.7} />
```
- `side` moves the voice button; `tilt` rotates (deck uses 7.6°, −7.7°, 16.7°, −14.3°).
- `GlassVoiceButton` is exported standalone.
